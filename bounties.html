PyHealth Bounties

Build PyHealth with us

Bounties are models, datasets, tasks and tools we'd love help adding. Claim one by opening a pull request with [BTY-<id>] in its title. When it merges, your name is listed here as the person who built it.

Why contribute: students in the DL4H course can earn extra credit, and contributors who complete a bounty may get the chance to join a research paper and work with our industry partners.
