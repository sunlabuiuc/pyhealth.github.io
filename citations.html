How this list was made

Citing papers were collected from Semantic Scholar and OpenAlex in October 2026, plus full-text searches for “PyHealth”. Each paper’s text was checked for how it uses PyHealth. Institutions come from OpenAlex author affiliations, and include every co-author’s listed institution. Missing a paper, or spotted a mistake? Open an issue.
